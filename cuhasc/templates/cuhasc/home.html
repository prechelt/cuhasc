1. How to use

  • Create a Team.
  • Send the resulting members link to the team's members (typically by email).
  • Have each team member fill in the culture questionnaire in the language they know best.
  • Visit the team page. It will show the team's culture profile and show all those sections from the handbook that apply to a team with this profile.
  • Read those handbook sections together with the team and discuss how to apply them.
  • By applying the handbook sections' advice, overcome that part of your process execution issues that is due to culture effects.

To find the team page again, you need to remember its URL. It is also stored in a cookie and will come up automatically on the home page for as long as that cookie lives. If you lose the cookie and the URL, visit the adminpage (shown on the console upon 'cuhasc run') for retrieving them.
